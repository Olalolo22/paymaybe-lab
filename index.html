    What happens when you pay

    Four steps, each one a transaction you can look up.

    1. Your payment is held

      The price goes into the store's vault on Solana. Nothing is refunded or kept until your spin settles.

    2. Your spin goes private

      Your checkout moves into a private MagicBlock rollup. While it's there, nobody else can read it.

    3. A number is drawn

      MagicBlock VRF draws a number from 0 to 9,999. Nobody can choose it, the store included.

    4. The result is settled

      The result is written back to Solana. Claim your cashback or sell shards whenever you like.

      Why nobody can rig it
      
            
      The number comes from MagicBlock's verifiable random function. The oracle signs it, the program checks the signature before it pays anything, and the request includes a seed from your wallet. The store can't pick a number and neither can you.
      
            
      The odds above are written into the store's account when it opens, so they can be checked on-chain.
